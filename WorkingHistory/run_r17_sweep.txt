WHERE THE DISPERSION COMES FROM: GRINDING, MEASURED
==============================================================================
  Same circuit, 74 queries at blowup 16, 24 interleaved pairs. Only the
  grinding bits differ. Every proof verifies: true.

  grinding bits                min        q1    median        q3     q3-q1
  ---------------------- --------- --------- --------- --------- ---------
  0                        5294.89   5336.57   5367.30   5397.59     61.02
  20                       5419.92   5681.13   5977.17   6846.59   1165.46

  median difference 609.87 ms, spread difference 1104.44 ms.
  A geometric search over 2^20 hashes has a mean and a standard
  deviation of the same order, so if grinding is the source these two
  differences should be comparable and the 0-bit row should be tight.
