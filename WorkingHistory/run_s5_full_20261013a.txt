  The smallest proof measured here is 2789121 bytes at 28 provable bits,
  which is 21.3 blobs. No setting in this sweep brings a proof of this
  circuit within one block of call data: the opening proof dominates and
  it shrinks only with the query count, which is what soundness buys.
  A backend meeting a settlement domain's budget has to replace the
  opening argument, not tune it.
  Soundness gaps closed: C6 uses the field constant one in-circuit
  rather than a witness adjustment; share indices are forced non-zero
  by a witnessed inverse; and each sponge carries every lane its
  chunk does not absorb, not only the capacity, so a partial final
  chunk leaves no rate lane for a prover to choose.
